**Descripción:** Fast 60-second Etsy shop scorecard that gives a seller a single Shop Health Score out of 100 and their top 3 sales blockers. Use this skill whenever an Etsy seller wants a quick read on how healthy their shop is, asks "how is my shop doing", wonders why they get views but no sales, or wants a fast first opinion before a full audit. Produces one overall score, a color band, the three biggest blockers in real Etsy terms, and one action to take today. Trigger this for any quick Etsy shop check, scorecard, health score, or first-look diagnosis — even if the seller only describes a symptom like "lots of views but no orders" rather than asking for a "scorecard" by name.
