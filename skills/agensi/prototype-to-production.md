**Descripción:** Takes a vibe-coded prototype (Lovable, Bolt, v0 or similar export) and makes it deployable by moving secrets to env, adding input validation, basic auth, backups, a healthcheck, secure headers, and plain-language deploy notes for a single VPS. Use when the user wants to make a prototype production-ready, deploy a Lovable/Bolt/v0 app, harden a vibe-coded app, add real auth and backups, or ship a prototype safely.

**Cuándo usar:** Turn a prototype into something deployable. The audience is a **non-technical owner**: every output you show them must be plain language, say "your app's password" not "credential", say "a copy of your data" not "snapshot retention policy".
