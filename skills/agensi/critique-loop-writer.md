**Descripción:** Write polished prose through an iterative self-critique loop with background research on genre, audience, and best practices. Draft the piece, then scrutinize it as a specific skeptical reader, revising until the reviewer signs off. Use for essays, articles, blog posts, speeches, eulogies, toasts, personal statements, creative writing, persuasive pieces, reviews, or op-eds — including casual requests like "help me write this and make it good" or "draft this and tear it apart." Do NOT use for cover letters, job emails, Suno lyrics, or brief messages.

**Cuándo usar:** A writing skill that produces tight, human-sounding prose by running every draft past a specific skeptical reader, revising, and repeating until that reader has nothing substantive left to complain about.
