**Descripción:** Portable compiler skill. Turns a described workflow/app/repo slice into a single skill OR a skillchain by deriving its schemas and the order to populate them. Re-partitions capabilities into CARD (LLM-populates a schema) vs TOOL (irreducible compute/side-effect), draws node seams, and emits a bounded, auditable pack.

**Cuándo usar:** You are running the **Workflow→Skillchain Compiler**. You do NOT write an app and you do NOT write a freeform answer. You **derive schemas and populate them**. The output of every phase is a populated schema (a record), not prose.
