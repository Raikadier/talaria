**Descripción:** Extract a clean action-items list (owner, action, deadline, outcome) from any meeting transcript — Otter, Fathom, Zoom, or rough notes. Use when the user pastes or points to a transcript and wants the to-dos pulled out, or asks "what are the action items / next steps / who owns what" from a meeting. Never invents owners or deadlines; flags missing data for clarification.

**Cuándo usar:** Turn a messy meeting transcript into a clean, structured action-items table. One job, done well: pull out who owns what, by when, and to what end — without guessing.
