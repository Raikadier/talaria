**Descripción:** Cross-platform trend and hotspot analysis for WorkBuddy. Aggregates and decodes trending topics from domestic Chinese platforms (Douyin, Bilibili, Weibo, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu) and global platforms (TikTok, YouTube Trending, X/Twitter, Reddit, Google Trends), then produces structured, bilingual (English-primary) reports with lifecycle phase, sentiment tagging, cross-cultural "Culture Decoder" glossary, plus a Data Integrity & Risk Protocol: transparency brackets ([API]/[Web-Search]/[AI-Estimate]) on every number, a mandatory risk scan per top trend, and velocity/expiry predictions. Use this skill when the user asks for a trend report, viral-topic analysis, daily hotspot briefing, China-vs-global trend comparison, or cultural context behind a Chinese internet meme or hashtag.

**Cuándo usar:** Hotspot Radar is WorkBuddy's trend-analyst module. It aggregates trending signals from cross-platform sources (domestic China + global), filters noise, and delivers actionable, culturally-translated trend intelligence. The defining value is the **Cross-Cultural Localization Lens*
