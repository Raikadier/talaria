**Descripción:** Generate a blended, day-by-day wedding ceremony agenda for a cross-cultural or interfaith couple. Takes each partner's religion, community/subtradition, and country, plus the number of days the celebration will span, and produces a thoughtful multi-format output — an interactive HTML timeline, a clean markdown outline, a printable Word doc for officiants and venues, and a guest-facing PDF share card. Use this skill whenever someone is planning an interfaith, intercultural, cross-country, or multi-religion wedding; whenever they mention needing to blend two sets of traditions, combine rituals from two families, plan an agenda or schedule or itinerary or timeline for a ceremony, honor both sides' customs, or draft a run-of-show across multiple days — even if they don't explicitly say "agenda." Also use when the user references the Sangam app, the wedding-agenda design concept, or asks to generate a ceremony schedule for specific couples.

**Cuándo usar:** This skill generates a blended, day-by-day ceremony agenda for a couple marrying across religions, communities, or countries. The output honors both traditions with equal weight and is rendered in whichever formats the user asks for (HTML, markdown, .docx, PDF).
