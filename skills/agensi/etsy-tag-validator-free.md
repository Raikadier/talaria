**Descripción:** Validate a buyer's 13 Etsy tags against Etsy's rules and grade their search quality. Use when a seller pastes their current Etsy listing tags and wants them checked for rule compliance (13 max, 20 characters each, no commas) and quality (multi-word, long-tail, buyer-intent). Produces a validation report, a quality score, and the top 5 replacement tag suggestions. Free funnel edition — routes to the paid Etsy Listing Optimizer for a full listing rewrite.

**Cuándo usar:** Grade and fix a seller's 13 Etsy tags. This Skill validates against Etsy's hard rules, scores the tag set for search quality, and proposes the 5 highest-impact replacements. It does **not** rewrite the whole listing — that is the paid upgrade (see the callout at the end).
