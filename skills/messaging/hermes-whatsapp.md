**Descripción:** Configure, send, and troubleshoot the WhatsApp Baileys bridge in Hermes (built-in `hermes whatsapp` pairing + `hermes send --to whatsapp:`). Covers the E.164 country-code requirement, why messages silently fail, allowlist reload gotchas, and why the bot does not reply to inbound messages.

**Cuándo usar:** Hermes ships a WhatsApp integration via a local bridge (`hermes whatsapp`) that pairs your personal WhatsApp account by QR and exposes it for `hermes send` and the inbound gateway.
