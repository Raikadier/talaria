**Descripción:** Given a topic to learn, find the BEST YouTube video(s) on it using expert-consensus triangulation (not view counts or recommendations), to avoid misinformation. Use when the user wants to learn something from video, asks "what is the best video to learn X", or needs a trustworthy source on YouTube for a topic. Reuses the scientific method from book-finder but adapted to the higher misinformation risk of YouTube.

**Cuándo usar:** - "quiero aprender X, ¿qué video de YouTube veo?" - "necesito un video confiable sobre Y" - el usuario quiere evitar desinformación en YouTube (clickbait, gurus, conspiracy)
